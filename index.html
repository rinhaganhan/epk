Audiovisual Concept

The Refraction of Sound and Light

"Eternal Light" is a conceptual Melodic Techno project conceived from an original sci-fi narrative written by the artist. The sonic experience emulates the physical behavior of an optical prism: an initial pure light that refracts into distinct chromatic frequencies through each track, culminating in a high-energy melodic climax.

"Each composition represents a specific wavelength of the visible spectrum. Analog synths, deep basslines, and melodic progressions construct an immersive journey designed specifically for mainstages and festival crowds."

Biography & Creative Vision

Behind the Project

I'm a software engineer by profession, but music has been part of my life since I was 15. Over the years I've written and produced music across different genres, and electronic music eventually led me to start this project.

I'm also a longtime admirer of EDM and have had the opportunity to witness different live shows and DJs in person. Seeing the scale of the music, visuals, and storytelling firsthand was genuinely inspiring and pushed me to finally complete a project I had been developing for quite some time.

The result is Eternal Light, a seven-track conceptual electronic music album inspired by the seven visible colors of a prism. Each track represents a different stage of a larger journey exploring memory, identity, time, fractured realities, and transformation. Every song has its own atmosphere, sound, and emotional identity, while remaining part of the same narrative.

7 COLORS • 7 FRAGMENTS • 1 JOURNEY

The project is also connected to a larger original sci-fi universe based on a novel I wrote and published last year, El Efecto Prisma. The music was developed as another way of exploring that universe: through sound, emotion, and atmosphere rather than words alone.

I created this project independently and put a great deal of myself into it. I led the production process: creative direction, concept, narrative, lyrics, artistic decisions, and overall project identity were developed by me.

Through this EPK, I wanted to share the complete project because all seven tracks were designed to work together as a single journey. Of course, I would be completely open to collaborations, development, or exploring individual tracks if something connects with other EDM projects.

If I was able to build this universe independently, I can only imagine what could happen by working with people who share a similar vision for music, technology, and immersive experiences.
